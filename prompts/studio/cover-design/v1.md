tier = "writer"

Design one finished, scroll-stopping social media cover for a business audience. ${slide_note}

Format: ${orientation}. ${safe_area}

Background: a candid documentary photograph, not a render and not stock art. ${scene} Any paper, notebook, screen, whiteboard, poster or sign in the scene must be blank, blurred or turned away: nothing in the scene may carry readable words, letters or numbers. Camera and light: ${shot}. Natural light, small real-life imperfections, believable people and places, natural expressions, correct hands. The emotion should make a busy owner stop and want to know more: curiosity or mild concern, never panic. House style: ${style}

Typography. The text is part of the design and must be spelled exactly as written, letter for letter.
Headline, exactly: "${headline}"
Set the headline large (not gigantic: the whole text block takes at most the top 45 percent of the picture) in a heavy, modern sans-serif, left aligned, in two or three short lines, with high contrast against the picture. Set the one or two most important words in a bright accent colour and the rest in white or near white. Put a subtle dark gradient or a solid panel behind the text so it reads instantly on a phone.
${support}
Write no other words anywhere in the image: no extra labels, stickers, arrows with notes, handwriting, slogans, numbers, logos, watermarks, and no text on objects, screens, mugs, boards or signs.

Accent colours taken from the brand: ${palette}. Use them for the highlighted words and one small graphic accent only.
Composition: one clear focal point, generous calm space around the text, strong contrast, uncluttered, readable at thumbnail size.

Do not include: glossy plastic skin; perfect symmetry; neon glow; futuristic holograms; floating icons; robots; glowing brains; padlock or shield clip art; stock photo handshakes; over-saturated colours; cartoon or 3D render look; hooded hackers. ${avoid}
