Candid documentary photograph. ${scene}

Camera and light: ${shot}. Natural available light, true to the place, with the small imperfections of a real photo: slight grain, uneven shadows, a little clutter, nothing posed or perfectly centred. Real materials and worn surfaces. Composition: the subject sits in the upper two thirds of the frame; the lower third is calm and uncluttered, because a headline is placed there afterwards. Ordinary people dressed for the setting, natural expressions, correct hands. ${orientation} framing.

House style: ${style}

Colour hints taken from the brand: ${palette}. Let them appear naturally in objects or clothing; do not tint the whole image.

Do not include: any text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or signs; glossy plastic skin; perfect symmetry; neon glow; futuristic holograms; floating icons; robots; glowing brains; stock photo handshakes; over-saturated colours; cartoon or 3D render look. ${avoid}
