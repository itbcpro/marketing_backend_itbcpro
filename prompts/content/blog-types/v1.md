## ultimate_guide
A pillar resource that fully explains a topic for someone new and still teaches an expert something. Informational intent. Structure: definition and why it matters now, how it works, the main options or components, how to choose, step-by-step implementation, costs and effort, common mistakes, advanced considerations, tools and checklists, what to do next. Builds topical authority; link out to every supporting article that exists.

## how_to
A task the reader wants to complete. Informational and problem-solving intent. Structure: outcome and prerequisites, time and cost, numbered steps with the reason for each, troubleshooting by symptom, how to verify it worked, mistakes to avoid, variations for different situations. Steps are specific and ordered.

## listicle
A ranked or grouped set of items (tools, tips, mistakes, examples). Commercial investigation or informational intent. Each item gets a real reason to be on the list, who it suits, a limit or trade-off, and one concrete detail. State the selection criteria before the list. No padding items; fewer strong items beat many weak ones. Never rank as "best" without stating the criteria.

## comparison
A decision between named options. Commercial investigation intent. Structure: short verdict by use case first, comparison table on the criteria that matter, one section per criterion, cost over time, who should pick which, switching costs, honest weaknesses of each, and when neither fits. Be fair and make no unsupported claims about third parties.

## case_study
A specific project and its result. Use only facts the company supplied in the brief or brand profile; if there is no real case material, list that in `needs_source` and write a clearly labelled scenario ("a typical project") instead of a fake client story. Structure: situation, constraints, options considered, what was done, the result with the measured numbers provided, lessons, what to do differently.

## faq_answer
A question-led article that answers one broad question and its neighbours. Informational intent, built for featured snippets and AI answers. Each H2 is a real question with a 40 to 80 word direct answer, then depth. Group questions by stage: basics, choosing, doing, costs, problems, advanced.

## trend_news
A timely topic and what it means for the reader. Informational intent with freshness. State what changed and when (only facts that can be verified or are marked as needing a source), why it matters, who is affected, what to do now, what to watch. Mark every time-sensitive claim and state the review date in `freshness_notes`.

## opinion
A reasoned position from the company's point of view. Thought leadership. Structure: the claim, the common view and why it falls short, the evidence and reasoning, objections answered fairly, where the position does not hold, what the reader should do. Opinions are labelled as such and argued, not asserted.

## checklist_template
A reusable checklist, template or framework the reader can copy. Informational, high utility. Provide the artefact itself early (checklist, table or template), then explain each item: why it is there, how to do it, how to check it. Include a version for beginners and a short advanced add-on.

## local_service
A service explained for a place. Local and commercial intent. Cover what the service is, who needs it locally, local conditions that matter (regulation, climate, market; typical costs only if supplied), how to choose a provider, process and timeline, questions to ask, and a relevant call to action. Mention the city or region naturally; never stuff place names.

## glossary
A definition-led article that owns a term. Informational intent. Open with a one-sentence definition and a 40 to 80 word explanation, then context, how it works, examples, related terms, how it differs from similar terms, common misunderstandings, and where it appears in practice.
