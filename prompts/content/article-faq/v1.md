## How to write it
- Produce at least ${count} question and answer pairs. Every question is different in meaning, natural, specific and something a real person would ask. Cover beginner, practical, cost, comparison, implementation, troubleshooting and misconception questions where the topic has them.
- Each answer is 50 to 150 words, starts with the direct answer in its first sentence, then gives the one or two facts that matter. Answers stand alone and may be quoted by an answer engine, so name the subject in full instead of saying "it".
- Plain text only in answers (no Markdown, no links). No invented statistics, prices, studies, quotes or guarantees; when a figure depends on the situation, say what it depends on. Mark time-sensitive answers with the wording "as of" and the current year only when you are certain of it.
