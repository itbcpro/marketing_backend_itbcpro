## How to review
- If the owner gave a primary keyword, judge the page against it and do not replace it; otherwise work out the one primary keyword a visitor would type to find this page. Base it on the page itself, not on guesses about volume. Take the audience, country, region and goal into account when judging the intent match, tone and calls to action. List up to four secondary keywords the page already supports.
- `summary` is two or three plain sentences: what the page is, how well it serves that intent, the single biggest improvement. `verdict` is one of strong, good, needs_work, weak.
- `strengths`: up to four real strengths, each backed by something visible on the page.
- `problems`: up to eight, most important first. Each has a severity, the area (title, description, structure, content depth, trust, links, intent match, readability, AI answers), the evidence you saw on the page, and the fix. Do not list a problem the automatic checks already cover unless you add new evidence or a better fix.
- `proposals` are changes the owner can apply with one click. Allowed fields: `seo_title` (about 50 to 60 characters, primary keyword near the front, brand last if it fits), `meta_description` (about 140 to 158 characters, says what the visitor gets, ends with a reason to click, no quotation marks), `title` (the page heading), `slug` (short, lower case, hyphens, only if the current one is clearly poor and the page is not already ranking). Propose a field only when your version is clearly better than the current text; keep the company's voice and facts. Use `advice` for anything that is not a field change (rewrite the introduction, add a section, add a table, merge with another page). Each proposal has a reason and an impact (high, medium, low). At most one proposal per field except advice.
- `faq`: up to five questions a real visitor asks about this topic that the page does not answer yet, each with a short, factual answer written only from what the page or brand profile supports. If you cannot answer from the given material, leave the answer empty rather than inventing it.
- `internal_links`: up to five links from this page to the listed neighbors, each with natural anchor text and why it helps. Use only addresses from the neighbor list.
- `geo_tips`: up to four tips so AI answer engines can quote the page (answer first in the opening lines, question style headings, short definitions, lists or tables, clear author or company identity, dates, sources).
- When search numbers are available, use them as evidence: queries with many impressions but few clicks point at a weak title or description; queries at positions 8 to 20 are the best chances to improve; a falling trend deserves an explanation. Say which numbers you used. When they are not available, do not guess rankings or traffic.
- Never invent statistics, prices, guarantees, awards, customer names or sources. Never claim a ranking or traffic number: you do not have them. Write in plain English for a non-expert.
