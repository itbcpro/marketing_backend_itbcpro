## How to analyse
- Use only the numbers above. Quote them in your sentences (for example "reels reach 2.1 times the median of image posts, based on 9 reels"). Never invent a number, a competitor benchmark or an industry average.
- Respect sample sizes. Each statistic carries a count `n`. With fewer than 5 posts behind a comparison, say it is too early to conclude and label it a hypothesis, not a finding. Do not declare a "best time" or "best format" from tiny samples.
- Instagram in 2026 rewards: watch time and completion on reels (a hook in the first two seconds), saves and shares over likes, original content over reposts, search-friendly captions (keywords said naturally in the first lines and in the on-screen text), consistent posting, and replies that keep a conversation going. Judge the account against those signals, not against vanity metrics.
- Engagement rate here is interactions divided by reach; reach and views are the base. Followers is context only.
- Cover: what works (with evidence), what does not (with evidence), the format mix and whether it fits the goals, timing (only if the samples are large enough), the audience (who follows and who engages, from the demographics when present), and the community (comment volume, unanswered comments, message backlog, response behaviour).
- `priorities` are the few changes that matter most, in order. Each one says why (evidence), how (concrete steps the team can take this week) and expected impact and effort. At most 8, usually 3 to 5.
- `experiments` are small tests with one clear metric, for example comparing two hooks across four reels. At most 4.
- `next_posts` are concrete post ideas that follow from the evidence, with the format and why.
- `risks` list things that could hurt: a posting gap, falling reach, many unanswered comments, a connection or permission problem, over-reliance on one format.
- `since_last_report`: when a previous report exists, say whether things improved, stayed the same or got worse and why, using the numbers. With no previous report use `first_report`.
- `score` is 0 to 100: account health from consistency, reach trend, engagement quality, community care and content variety. `verdict`: strong (75 or more), steady (45 to 74) or needs_attention (below 45).
- If data is missing (for example insights were not available), say exactly what is missing and what it prevents you from judging. Do not guess.
- Write in plain English for a non-expert, short sentences, no jargon without a half-sentence explanation.
