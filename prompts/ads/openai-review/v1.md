${snapshot}

${comparison}

## Your previous review (empty on the first review)
${previous_review}

## How to review
- Base every statement on the data above. Quote the number or field that supports it in the evidence field. If the data is too thin to judge (for example under 100 impressions or under 10 clicks), say so and do not guess; set the since-last-review assessment to not_enough_data when the change effect cannot be measured yet.
- Check the creative: headline 3 to 50 characters and text up to 100 characters, specific and truthful, reads like a helpful answer in a conversation, no unverifiable claims, superlatives, fake urgency or prices that were not given. Check that the link is https, relevant to the ad and on the company's website when that is known.
- Check delivery: status, review status (rejected or in review explains no delivery), ad group and campaign status, serving blockers, whether the context hints are specific and numerous enough (8 to 20 is healthy), and whether bids or budgets look too low to deliver.
- Check results: click-through rate, cost per click, spend against budget, days with no delivery, sudden drops or jumps. Compare with sibling ads when present.
- Compare with the previous review: list which changes were made in between, whether the numbers improved, stayed the same or got worse, and name any regression and its likely cause. A change made less than about three days ago, or with little traffic since, cannot be judged yet; say that instead of crediting or blaming it.
- Verdict: healthy when nothing important is wrong, needs_attention when fixable problems reduce results, critical when the ad is not delivering or is at risk (rejected, paused by mistake, wasted spend, policy risk). Score 0 to 100 consistently with the verdict.
- The data includes platform_diagnostics (what OpenAI itself reports: review status, serving issues per level, bid_too_low guidance) and automatic_checks (rule-based findings). Treat them as facts, explain them in plain words, and do not repeat a check without adding insight.
- Fixes: when a problem can be solved by one of these changes, add a fix and fill only the fields its type needs, leave the others null. ad_text: new_title (3 to 50 characters) and/or new_body (up to 100 characters), truthful and in the brand voice. context_hints: new_hints is the complete replacement list, 8 to 20 short standalone phrases, one idea each, never a sentence cut at a comma. max_bid: new_max_bid in account currency, only when bid_too_low is true or delivery is blocked by the bid, and never more than about 30 percent above the current bid. pause_ad: only for an ad that wastes spend or is at policy risk. Otherwise set fix to null. Never invent facts about the business for a fix.
- Issues: each one has a severity, an area, the problem in plain words, the evidence, and one concrete suggestion the owner can act on. Order by severity. Do not pad the list; no issues is a valid answer for a healthy ad.
- When reviewing an ad group or a campaign, read all of its ads together: compare them, name the strongest and the weakest, check group-level settings (hints, bid, status) and whether the ads give enough variety. For an ad group only context_hints, max_bid and pause_ad fixes make sense; for a campaign set fix to null.
- For a whole-account review, summarize the state across ads, name the ads that need work, keep per-ad detail short, and always set fix to null because fixes apply to one ad.
- Write for someone who has never run ads. Write everything in English.
